# Day 02 – Sales Orders Data Analysis

## Project Overview
As part of my 30-Day Advanced Excel + AI learning journey, I worked with a sales orders dataset to understand sales performance and business information using Microsoft Excel.

## Dataset Information
The dataset contains 15 sales records and 10 columns:

- Order_ID
- Employee
- Product
- Order_Date
- Sales
- Discount
- Quantity
- Region
- Payment_Mode
- Status

## Analysis Objectives
- Identify employees appearing most frequently.
- Find products with the highest individual sales.
- Compare regional orders.
- Identify pending and completed orders.
- Analyze discounts.
- Understand sales patterns across products and employees.

## Excel Skills Practised
- Sorting
- Filtering
- Basic data analysis
- Comparing values
- Identifying patterns and trends
- Answering business questions using raw data

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- AI assistance

## Learning Outcome
Practised exploring raw sales data, identifying important business information and answering business-related questions through data analysis.

# Day 04 – Advanced Excel + AI Practice 📊

## 📌 Learning Objectives

Today I continued my 30-day Advanced Excel + AI learning journey, focusing on data organization, sorting, filtering and Named Ranges.

## 📚 Topics Covered

- **Sorting Data:** Arranging sales data from Largest to Smallest and A to Z.
- **Filtering Data:** Displaying specific records based on conditions.
- **Named Ranges:** Assigning meaningful names to cell ranges.
- **Data Cleaning:** Correcting inconsistent employee name capitalization.
- **Business Analysis:** Identifying top-performing employees, regions and products.

## 🛠️ Practical Tasks

1. Converted raw sales data into an Excel Table using `Ctrl + T`.
2. Cleaned inconsistent employee names.
3. Sorted sales from Largest to Smallest.
4. Filtered Pending orders.
5. Filtered Laptop sales.
6. Created a Named Range called `Sales_Data`.
7. Calculated total sales using:

```excel
=SUM(Sales_Data)
```

## 📊 Business Insights

- Highest sales employee: Rahul
- Highest sales region: West
- Pending orders: 5
- Highest revenue product: Laptop
- Completed Laptop orders: 301 and 310

## 🤖 AI Practice

Used ChatGPT to explore:
- Data-cleaning techniques.
- Sorting and filtering instructions.
- Identifying business insights.
- Understanding sales analysis.

## 💡 Key Learning

I learned that sorting and filtering help organize large datasets and Named Ranges make formulas easier to understand.

I also learned the importance of aggregating employee sales before identifying the highest-performing employee.
